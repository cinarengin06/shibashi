# Design Guidelines

- Digital Tai Chi dojo; not a fitness or meditation dashboard
- Apple Human Interface Guidelines
- Mobile-first, minimal, cinematic and calm
- One primary action per screen
- No glassmorphism overload and no pure black
- Dark stone, mist, ivory, gold and soft bronze
- Cormorant Garamond + Inter
- 5 Shen changes atmosphere, motion, shapes and micro-interactions
- Breath-paced motion: 600 / 900 / 1200 / 1600 ms, ease-in-out
- Maximum 60 FPS and reduced-motion support
- Haptic only at breath start, posture completion and day completion
- Progressive disclosure: Arrival, Practice, Awareness, Mastery
- Never show demo scores or unavailable features as active

Visual references are the highest priority. Match their overall design language, typography, spacing, component hierarchy, button shapes, icon style, premium feeling and atmosphere. Do not copy pixel-by-pixel, but keep the same design DNA.

If the implementation and the screenshots conflict, prioritize the screenshots for visual design and the existing app for interaction behavior.
